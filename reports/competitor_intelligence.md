# Competitor Sentiment & Voice of Customer Intelligence Report

This report outlines comparative insights between Nykaa Cosmetics and Kay Beauty based on topic modeling of customer reviews and feedback.

---

## 1. What is Nykaa Cosmetics Doing Better?
* **Key Praise Areas**: Praised for mapped categories such as **Product Quality, Value For Money**.
* **Digital Advocacy**: Strong customer loyalty is driven by product efficacy and brand experience.

## 2. What is Kay Beauty Doing Better?
* **Key Praise Areas**: Praised for mapped categories such as **Product Quality, Visible Results**.
* **Strengths**: Customers report high satisfaction indices for daily mild skincare cleansers.

## 3. What Complaints are Unique to Each Brand?
* **Unique Kay Beauty Complaints**: Breakouts
* **Unique Nykaa Cosmetics Complaints**: Delivery Issues, Strong Fragrance

## 4. What Opportunities Exist for Improvement?
* **For Nykaa Cosmetics**: Address top complaints: Packaging Issues, Product Ineffectiveness.
* **For Kay Beauty**: Address top complaints: Price Concerns, Product Ineffectiveness.
