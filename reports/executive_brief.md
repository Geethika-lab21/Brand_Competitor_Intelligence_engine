# Executive Briefing: Brand Sentiment & Competitor Intelligence

**Audience**: Skincare Executive Board & Business Stakeholders  
**Scope**: Voice of Customer Analysis & Strategic Product Recommendations

---

## 1. Key Findings
* **Competitive Divide**: Nykaa Cosmetics leads digital advocacy due to specific category satisfaction. Kay Beauty receives positive marks for botanical daily products, but faces higher negative ratios on certain items.
* **Core Product Issues**: Customer complaints center heavily on packaging durability (droppers/pumps) and product texture.

---

* **Delivery Issues**: 4 consumer complaints

* **Packaging Issues**: 36 consumer complaints

* **Product Ineffectiveness**: 1078 consumer complaints
* **Skin Irritation**: 60 consumer complaints
* **Strong Fragrance**: 47 consumer complaints
* **Unmapped**: 36 consumer complaints

* **Product Quality**: 34435 positive mentions
* **Repurchase Intent**: 720 positive mentions
* **Skin Hydration**: 1037 positive mentions
* **Unmapped**: 197 positive mentions
* **Value For Money**: 1600 positive mentions

---

## 4. Competitor Strengths
* **Nykaa Cosmetics**: High customer trust and clear active labeling.
* **Kay Beauty**: Refreshing daily cleansers and botanical formulation scents.

---

## 5. Competitor Weaknesses
* **Nykaa Cosmetics**: Issues with product texture and cosmetic application.
* **Kay Beauty**: Customer complaints highlight specific product quality concerns.

---

## 6. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Formulation Reformation**: Address cosmetic texture and application concerns.
2. **Clinical Formulation Audit**: Audit quality control on poorly performing product lines.
3. **Packaging Durability**: Upgrade dispensers, caps, and pumps to prevent leakage.
